Compound Yield Optimization & Inflation Drag Mechanics

Building high-yield liquidity balances safety with inflation defense. Keeping capital in low-yield traditional savings guarantees a silent loss in purchasing power, whereas optimized compound interest creates risk-free balance sheet expansion:

Daily Compounding APY Equation 
A = P * (1 + r/n)^(n*t)

Where:
  A = Terminal cash balance
  P = Initial liquid reserve
  r = Annual nominal interest rate
  n = Compounding periods per year (365 for daily compounding)
  t = Number of years elapsed
