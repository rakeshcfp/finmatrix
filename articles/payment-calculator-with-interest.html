Mathematical Debt Avalanche vs. Behavioral Snowball Mechanics

Eliminating multi-account debt requires choosing between purely mathematical interest minimization (Avalanche) and psychological momentum maximization (Snowball):

Debt Roll-Over Acceleration Mechanics 
Avalanche Strategy: Rank all debts by descending APR (Highest % First)
Snowball Strategy: Rank all debts by ascending balance (Smallest $ First)

Roll-Over Acceleration (Month t):
Payment on Target Account = Minimum Payment + Extra Prepayment + Sum(Minimums of Paid-Off Debts)

While the Avalanche method mathematically minimizes total interest charges, the Snowball method produces quick psychological wins by clearing smaller balances early.
