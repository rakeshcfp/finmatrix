Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

At Finmatrix ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible from https://finmatrix.cloud, the privacy of our visitors is our paramount priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and how we use it.

2. 100% Client-Side Private Computation

All numbers, income figures, expenses, loan balances, tax details, and personal scenarios entered into Finmatrix financial calculators are processed entirely client-side inside your web browser. We do NOT transmit, collect, log, or sell your financial calculator entries. Your sensitive financial data never leaves your device.

3. Cookies and Local Storage

Finmatrix uses minimal browser local storage strictly for functional preferences, such as remembering your Dark or Light theme selection and currency preferences.

4. Third-Party Links & Affiliate Partners

Finmatrix contains outbound links to financial institutions. When you click external partner links, those third parties operate under their respective privacy policies. Partner links use rel="sponsored nofollow noopener".
