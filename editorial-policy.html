Editorial Policy, Calculation Standards & FTC Disclosures

🎯 Our Core Editorial Principle: Calculation correctness is more important than feature volume. In personal finance (YMYL), incorrect numbers harm real people. Every calculator published on Finmatrix must disclose its assumptions, cite primary authoritative sources, and undergo automated mathematical unit testing.

1. How Calculations Are Built and Reviewed

Finmatrix tools are built strictly on standard financial engineering formulas and statutory tax code logic:

  • Standard Amortization & TVM: Loan and mortgage payment formulas utilize the standard annuity present-value formula: PMT = P * (r / (1 - (1 + r)^(-n))), matching the standards prescribed by Fannie Mae and the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB).
  • Debt Elimination Models: The Debt Avalanche and Snowball algorithms perform true monthly multi-debt amortization simulations, factoring in compounding interest, minimum monthly payment caps, and leftover payment cascade.
  • Tax Models: Federal income tax withholding is calculated using progressive bracket slices rather than effective tax rate simplifications. FICA withholding implements statutory wage caps for Social Security ($184,500 in 2026) and Additional Medicare surtaxes on high earners.
  • Independent Code Audits: Before any calculation formula is released, it is tested against known reference scenarios using automated unit test suites (`tests/calculator-formulas.test.js`), including edge cases such as 0% interest, zero balance, and high-net-worth brackets.

2. How and When Tax-Year Data Is Updated

Tax brackets, standard deduction amounts, retirement contribution caps, and FICA wage bases are updated annually according to the following strict schedule:

  • Annual Release Audits (October – November): When the IRS issues its official inflation adjustment Revenue Procedure (e.g., Rev. Proc. 2025-32 for Tax Year 2026) and the Social Security Administration publishes its annual Cost-of-Living bulletin, our quantitative team updates config/tax-year.json with exact statutory figures.
  • January Roll-Forward: On January 1st of each calendar year, all live calculators automatically roll forward to compute against the new tax year's verified parameters.
  • Verification Tagging: Every statutory constant is labeled with its official government source URL and a verification boolean. Any parameter pending final regulatory confirmation is explicitly tagged TODO: VERIFY and logged in our public documentation.

3. How Affiliate Relationships Work (FTC Compliance)

Finmatrix is a free financial utility supported in part by financial affiliate partnerships. To ensure absolute consumer transparency and compliance with Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines:

  • Zero Formula Bias: Financial partners cannot buy higher calculator rankings, manipulate formula outputs, or alter mathematical recommendations. If a tool indicates that paying off a 24% APR card via the Avalanche method is mathematically optimal, that result will never change based on commercial relationships.
  • Prominent Sponsorship Attributes: All partner and affiliate links explicitly declare rel="sponsored nofollow noopener" and open in a new window (target="_blank").
  • Contextual & Relevant: Partner offers appear contextually only after calculation results (e.g., balance transfer credit cards appear after debt calculation, high-yield savings accounts appear after emergency fund calculation).
  • No Artificial Scarcity or Phantom Offers: If an affiliate offer does not have a verified, active partner URL, no broken or deceptive link is shown to the user.

4. Corrections and Discrepancy Reporting

We invite user scrutiny. If you identify a computational bug or tax rule discrepancy, please email our editorial desk at novamatrix1564@gmail.com. Corrections are investigated, tested, and pushed to production within 48 hours.
