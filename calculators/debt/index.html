Debt Elimination & Credit Payoff Calculators

Carrying high-interest revolving credit card balances is one of the heaviest financial anchors facing American households. With national credit card APRs averaging above 21%, paying only minimum amounts can keep borrowers trapped in debt for decades. Our debt tools model the mathematical reality of Debt Avalanche (highest APR first) versus Debt Snowball (smallest balance first), calculate how extra monthly principal payments slash years off your payoff timeline, and quantify potential interest savings from 0% intro APR balance transfers.
