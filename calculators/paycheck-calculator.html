💡 Smart Money Breakdown: Key Concepts Explained in Plain English

💵 Gross Pay vs. Net Pay

Gross Pay is your total salary before deductions. Net Pay (Take-Home Pay) is the actual net cash deposited into your bank account after taxes and benefits.

🏥 FICA Tax Withholdings

Federal Insurance Contributions Act taxes consisting of Social Security (6.2%) and Medicare (1.45%) automatically deducted from employee paychecks.

🏦 Pre-Tax 401(k) Deductions

Contributions made to employer retirement plans before income taxes are calculated, lowering your current taxable income and annual tax bill.

📐 Paycheck Deductions Formula

Net Pay = Gross Pay - PreTax Deductions - Fed Tax - FICA (7.65%) - State Tax

  • Social Security Tax: 6.20% on income up to $176,100 (2025/2026 cap).
  • Medicare Tax: 1.45% on all earnings (+ 0.9% Additional Medicare tax over $200,000).

Worked Example: An annual gross salary of $85,000 paid biweekly yields approximately $2,450 net take-home pay per paycheck after standard withholding and a 5% 401(k) contribution.

2026 Statutory Federal & FICA Payroll Tax Architecture

Executive Summary: Net take-home pay is computed by deducting statutory Federal Income Tax brackets, Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA) withholding, and applicable state payroll taxes from gross salary, after subtracting eligible pre-tax elective deferrals.

  • Social Security (OASDI): 6.20% withholding levied on gross earnings up to the 2026 statutory wage cap of $176,100 (max annual employee contribution: $10,918.20). Earnings above this threshold incur 0% OASDI tax.
  • Medicare (HI): 1.45% withholding uncapped on all earnings, plus an additional 0.90% Additional Medicare Tax on wages exceeding $200,000 (Single) / $250,000 (Married Filing Jointly) under IRC § 3101(b).
  • Pre-Tax Elective Deferrals: 401(k)/403(b) contributions (up to $23,500 for 2025/2026) and HSA contributions ($4,300 Single / $8,550 Family) reduce Box 1 Federal Taxable Gross dollar-for-dollar.

Grounding Sources: IRS Revenue Procedure 2024-40; Social Security Administration 2026 Cost-of-Living & Wage Base Bulletin (42 U.S.C. § 430).

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How are FICA taxes calculated on my paycheck?

FICA taxes total 7.65% of gross earnings, comprising 6.2% for Social Security (up to the $176,100 wage base cap in 2025/2026) and 1.45% for Medicare.

Q: How do pre-tax 401(k) contributions lower my overall tax bill?

Pre-tax 401(k) contributions reduce your gross taxable income before federal and state income taxes are computed, directly lowering your immediate tax liability.
