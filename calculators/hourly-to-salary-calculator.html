How Hourly to Salary Is Calculated

In corporate payroll and human resources, the gold standard conversion between hourly wages and annual gross salaries is based on 2,080 working hours per year:

Standard Annual Hours: 40 Hours/Week × 52 Weeks = 2,080 Hours

Gross Annual Salary: Hourly Wage × 2,080 + (Overtime Hours × 1.5 × Hourly Wage × 52)

Net Take-Home: Gross Salary − [ 2026 Federal Tax + 7.65% FICA + State Tax ]

Quick Mental Rule of Thumb: To estimate your annual salary quickly in your head, double your hourly wage and add three zeros. For example, $35/hour × 2 = 70 → approximately $70,000/year ($72,800 precisely with 2,080 hours).

Worked Example: At $35.00/hour with zero overtime, your annual gross pay is $72,800. Under 2026 Single tax rules, you receive a $16,100 Standard Deduction, leaving $56,700 taxable federally ($6,214 tax). FICA is 7.65% ($5,569), and an estimated 5.0% state tax deducts $3,640. Total deductions equal $15,423, giving you a true net annual take-home of $57,377 ($2,207 every two weeks).

Assumptions & Official Sources

  • FLSA Compliance: Overtime is calculated at 1.5x base hourly wage for weekly hours beyond 40 per the U.S. Department of Labor Fair Labor Standards Act (DOL.gov/flsa).
  • Tax Year Standards: 2026 IRS marginal tax brackets and standard deductions ($16,100 Single / $32,200 MFJ) are verified against IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-32.
  • FICA Cap: Social Security tax is 6.2% up to the verified 2026 wage base of $184,500; Medicare is 1.45% uncapped.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many work hours are in a standard year?
A standard full-time year consists of 2,080 hours (40 hours per week × 52 weeks).
How do unpaid vacation days affect salary?
If you take 2 weeks of unpaid vacation, reduce the total weeks to 50 (2,000 hours). At $35/hour, 50 weeks equals $70,000 gross instead of $72,800.
How is overtime taxed?
Overtime pay is taxed as ordinary income. While your paycheck may have higher tax withheld due to temporary higher gross pay, your final tax rate at year-end is determined by your total annual taxable income.
