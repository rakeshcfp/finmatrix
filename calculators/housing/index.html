Housing, Mortgage & Real Estate Calculators

Purchasing a home or refinancing an existing mortgage is often the largest financial commitment in an individual's lifetime. Beyond the advertised loan interest rate, true housing costs encompass municipal property taxes, homeowners hazard insurance, and Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI). Our housing tools break down complete PITI payments, calculate maximum purchase prices using lender 28/36 debt-to-income benchmarks, evaluate the true opportunity cost of renting versus buying, and pinpoint your exact break-even timeline when considering a mortgage refinance.
